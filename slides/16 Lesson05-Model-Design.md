The next hypothesis concerns the structure of the subspace:

$$
W \subset \mathbb{R}^n
$$

to which the mean vector $\mu$ is assumed to belong.


This subspace is determined by the **design of the model**, which is formulated using independent variables associated with each measurement.


In our example, the independent variables are:

$$
\textbf{height }(h), \quad \textbf{weight }(w), \quad \textbf{age }(a)
$$
