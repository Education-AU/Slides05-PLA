title: Beyond Hypothesis Testing
template: default
---

The F-test allows us to **compare models**, but it is only one of many tools in statistical analysis.

After selecting a model, further analyses can include:

1. **Confidence intervals** for model parameters
2. **Prediction intervals** for future observations
3. **Model diagnostics** to check assumptions and assess fit
4. **Model selection criteria** (e.g., AIC, BIC)

**Key point:**

Hypothesis testing is just the first step. Statistical methods provide a wide range of tools to quantify uncertainty and
evaluate model quality.
