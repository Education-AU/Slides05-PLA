In our example the hypothesis concerning the mean vector $\mu$ might be formulated as follows
<div class="h3-blue">

$H_1$

</div>

Define vectors $\mathbf{b_1},\mathbf{b_2},\mathbf{b_3},\mathbf{b_4},\mathbf{b_5}$ in standard coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
b_1&=(1,1,\dots,1)\\
b_2&=(h_1,h_2,\dots,h_n)\\
b_3&=(w_1,w_2,\dots,w_n)\\
b_4&=(a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n)\\
b_5&=(a_1^2,a_2^2,\dots,a_n^2)
\end{aligned}
$$

Assume $\mathbf{b_i}$ are assumed linearly independent and that


$\mu\in \operatorname{span}({\mathbf{b_1},\mathbf{b_2},\mathbf{b_3},\mathbf{b_4},\mathbf{b_5}})$
