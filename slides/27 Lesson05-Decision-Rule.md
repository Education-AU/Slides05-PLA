---
title: Decision Rule for the F-Test
template: default
---

We now assess whether the restricted model hypothesis can be rejected or accepted relative to the full model.

1. If the test statistic is **small**, the residual sums of squares are similar, indicating that the additional
   variables in the full model do not improve the fit substantially.
2. If the test statistic is **large**, the reduction in residuals is too great to be explained by random variation
   alone.

**Decision:**

1. **Reject \(H_R\)** if the F-statistic is sufficiently large. This provides evidence that the restrictions imposed by
   the reduced model are not compatible with the data.
2. Otherwise, **fail to reject \(H_R\)**. The data do not provide sufficient evidence against the reduced model.
