How large must the test statistic be to reject $H_R$?  
This is determined by the **significance level** $\alpha$.

The critical value $F_{\text{crit}}$ of the $F$-distribution is defined by:

$$
P (F \ge F_{\text{crit}} \mid H_R \text{ true}) = \alpha
$$

or equivalently:

$$
P (F \le F_{\text{crit}} \mid H_R \text{ true}) = 1 - \alpha
$$

Common choices for $\alpha$ are $0.1$, $0.05$, or $0.01$, depending on the desired confidence.

**Interpretation:** $\alpha$ represents the probability of a **Type I error**—rejecting $H_R$ when it is actually true.
